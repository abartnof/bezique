# Period sources, kept as text

Plain-text copies of the two period treatises the rules lean on most, so that
a claim about what Foster or Cady says can be checked against Foster or Cady
without a network connection. Both works are in the public domain; the text is
the Internet Archive's OCR, with whitespace collapsed and nothing else changed.

| `foster-1897-bezique.txt` | R. F. Foster, *Foster's Complete Hoyle* (1897), pp. 337-345 | S47 |
| `cady-1896-bezique.txt` | A. Howard Cady, *Bézique* (1896), complete | S4 |

`docs/LITERATURE.md` is the full literature review, with every source and its URL.
