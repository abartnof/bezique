# Full code review, 2026-10-02

A review of the whole repository at `0ad58b8`, about 10,800 lines, made
before any long measurement run so that no run is spent on a defect that
could have been caught first (the maintainer's request). Six reviewers took
one area each and read every line of it: the rules core; declarations; the
hand, the game and observation; the opponent and its search (with the draft
of a rung 6); the measurement harness and every figure it supports; the
session, the protocol and the clients. Each reproduced its findings with a
scratch test or a command before reporting them.

The labels are those of the earlier reviews: **defect**, **latent defect**
(not reachable through the moves the engine offers), **document**, **test**,
**preference**, and here **design** for the rung-6 draft.

**In short:** no finding invalidates the rules, the scores, the views or the
measurements of rungs 1–5 as made. Independent checks found the trick rules,
the generator, the deal, the solver and the observation sound (among them,
127,843 view comparisons with hidden cards moved, and 3,000 endgames against
unpruned minimax). The findings that changed play were small (rollout memory
in rung 5's worlds; ties among declarations broken by copy number; one
missing declaration option), and are fixed; play is now `RECORD_VERSION` 2.
The measurement harness had weaknesses that would have hurt the long runs
(doubles invisible to the games measure, a mistyped seed silently reusing
the tuning deals, one core of two, an inflated statistic), now fixed. The
rung-6 draft was unsound and is to be redesigned.

Sound: the card encoding; parsing; the generator against its reference C,

streams (no two closer than about 3.5 × 10¹⁷); the deal against Foster
p. 339; trick-winning and the follow-and-win rule against independent
oracles (exhaustively for two-card hands); the exact odds in the deal test.
